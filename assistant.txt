MODULE 1: PROVISION THE ENTERPRISE AI MULTI-SERVICE HUB
================================================================================
WHAT THIS DOES:
Creates an umbrella "Azure AI Services" resource that activates Vision, Speech,
Language, OCR, and Document Intelligence under a single corporate endpoint.

STEP-BY-STEP IN AZURE PORTAL:
1. Open your browser and go to: https://portal.azure.com
2. In the top search bar, type: "Azure AI services" (or "Cognitive Services").
3. Click on "Azure AI services multi-service account" from the results.
4. Click the blue "+ Create" button.
5. Fill in the "Basics" tab:
   - Subscription : "Azure for Students"
   - Resource Group: Select your existing "rg-azure-explorer"
   - Region        : Select "Central India" (or "East US")
   - Name          : Enter a name (e.g., "ai-hub-explorer")
   - Pricing tier  : Standard S0
6. Review and Accept the Responsible AI terms checkbox.
7. Click "Review + create" at the bottom, then click "Create".
8. Wait ~60 seconds for deployment. Click "Go to resource".

WHAT TO INSPECT LIKE A PRO:
- In the left sidebar of your new resource, click "Keys and Endpoint".
- Notice:
  * "Endpoint": The base URL your client applications will call.
  * "KEY 1" and "KEY 2": Dual keys for rotation without zero downtime.

MODULE 2: AZURE AI FOUNDRY (STUDIO) & MODEL CATALOG
================================================================================
WHAT THIS DOES:
Azure AI Foundry (ai.azure.com) is the flagship workspace for AI Engineers.
Here you test models in real-time, tweak system instructions, and prototype.

STEP-BY-STEP IN BROWSER:
1. Open: https://ai.azure.com (sign in with your student account).
2. Look at the top left and select your directory/tenant:
   Tenant: 841e991f-1730-433f-82ba-ea7895004ffa
3. In the left navigation, click "Model catalog".
4. Browse through the available enterprise models:
   - Microsoft Phi-4 / Phi-3.5 (State-of-the-art small language models)
   - Meta Llama 3.3 (70B open weight instruction model)
   - Mistral Large & Codestral (Optimized for code generation)
   - OpenAI Models (GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini, text-embedding-3)

6. Click "Deploy" (Serverless API) or "Open in playground".
7. In the Chat Playground:
   - Set the "System message" (e.g., "You are an enterprise AI assistant for financial audit.").
   - Adjust "Temperature" (0 for deterministic code, 0.7 for creative writing).
   - Test prompting the model and watch the latency and token usage.

MODULE 3: ENTERPRISE RAG — AZURE AI SEARCH (VECTOR DATABASE)
================================================================================
WHAT THIS DOES:
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is the #1 project assigned to Forward
Deployed Engineers. Azure AI Search indexes company data and performs
hybrid search (BM25 keywords + vector embeddings + semantic ranking).

STEP-BY-STEP IN AZURE PORTAL:
1. Return to: https://portal.azure.com
2. In the top search bar, type: "AI Search" and click on "Azure AI Search".
3. Click "+ Create".
4. Fill in the "Basics" tab:
   - Subscription : "Azure for Students"
   - Resource Group: "rg-azure-explorer"
   - Service name  : Enter a unique name (e.g., "search-explorer-01")
   - Location      : "Central India" (or same region as your RG)
   - Pricing tier  : Click "Change Pricing Tier" -> Select "Free" (F)
     *(Note: Every Azure subscription gets 1 permanently Free search service!)*
5. Click "Review + create", then click "Create".
6. When deployment finishes, click "Go to resource".

PRO FDE FEATURE — "IMPORT AND VECTORIZE DATA" WIZARD:
1. On the overview page of your Search service, look at the top toolbar.
2. Notice the button: "Import and vectorize data".
3. This single wizard connects a Blob storage container containing enterprise
   PDFs, automatically chunks the text, runs an embedding model, and builds a
   searchable vector index ready for chat!

MODULE 4: DOCUMENT INTELLIGENCE STUDIO (ENTERPRISE OCR)
================================================================================
WHAT THIS DOES:
Enterprises have millions of scanned invoices, receipts, tax forms, and IDs.
FDEs use Document Intelligence to transform unsearchable scans into clean JSON.

STEP-BY-STEP IN BROWSER:
1. Open: https://documentintelligence.ai.azure.com
2. Click "Sign in" and connect your Azure AI Services resource from Module 1.
3. Explore the pre-built AI models:
   - "Read": Extracts printed and handwritten text.
   - "Layout": Extracts tables, headers, and structural reading order.
   - "Invoices & Receipts": Automatically extracts merchant, total, and tax.
4. Click "Invoices" -> Select one of the sample invoices.
5. Click "Run analysis".
6. Look at the right panel:
   - See how it detects "VendorName", "InvoiceTotal", "DueDate", and line items.
   - Click "JSON" tab to see the structured output your code will consume.

3. Click on your "Azure for Students" subscription.

   - Reset period: Monthly
   - Amount     : 20 (USD)
   - Alert %    : 80% (triggers when spend hits $16)
   - Email      : Your student email (23bcs061@iiitdmj.ac.in)
6. Click "Create". Now you will receive an alert long before credits run low!

STEP 5.2: Deleting the Resource Group When Finished
When you are completely done exploring and want to clean up:
1. Go to "Resource groups" -> Click "rg-azure-explorer".
2. Click "Delete resource group" in the top bar.
3. Type the resource group name to confirm, and click "Delete".
4. Everything inside is wiped out cleanly in one click.
